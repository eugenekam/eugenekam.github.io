- Two modes: interactive 3D "Enter the arena" plus a "Quick view" (/overview/) with everything on one scannable page. Works without JS via plain section links.
- Quick view order: hero + availability badge + CTAs, stat strip (4.0 GPA, 3 products, 10,000+ players, 100,000+ SKUs), numbered sections 01-05, contact.

- Honest data labelling: "Sample data", "Fictional demo data", "Live demo", "Used by Toledo Athletics staff".
- Filterable work index (All / Sports / Vision / Business), 15 numbered cards with tech tags.
- Clear positioning line and target roles; "Available now, open to relocation" badge; résumé PDF.
